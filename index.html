The Restaurant

Tapas no Pátio

23 Holland Street West, Bradford, Ontario

A family-run Portuguese restaurant in downtown Bradford, led by Portugal-born chef Luis Raimundo after decades cooking in Toronto. The menu is built around small plates for sharing (salt-cod fritters, cured presunto, seafood and grilled meats) in a room the house describes as warm and familiar.

Why Here

  • Portuguese cooking made for sharing across a table
  • Warm and familiar rather than formal
  • Quiet on a Wednesday: live entertainment is only on weekends
  • Open until midnight, so there's no rush
